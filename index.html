About

I’m a physicist exploring the fundamental laws of nature through high-energy particle physics, computational simulations, and data analysis. My current research focuses on quarkonium production in proton-proton collisions, using Monte Carlo event generators to study J/ψ production and compare simulations with LHC experimental data.

My research sits between two worlds. On one side, I work on reproducing ALICE and LHCb measurements of J/ψ production in proton–proton collisions at 13 TeV using the PYTHIA8 and PACIEA4 Monte Carlo generators, probing how multiplicity, transverse momentum, and multi-parton interactions shape charmonium production. On the other, my undergraduate dissertation examined how nanoparticle doping alters the electro-optical behaviour of nematic liquid crystals — work that connects soft-matter physics to practical display and biosensing applications.

Outside the lab, I've led my university's physics society, mentored students in STEM as a volunteer, and captained my university's karate team to two consecutive medals.

Research

From charmonium to liquid crystals

A working history of the projects and dissertations that have shaped my path through theoretical and experimental physics.

Present

Reproduction & Analysis of J/ψ Production Dynamics in pp Collisions

Advisor: Dr. Bibuti Parida · PYTHIA8 & PACIEA4
  • Reproducing published ALICE and LHCb measurements of J/ψ production at √s = 13 TeV using Monte Carlo event generators.
  • Investigating how charged-particle multiplicity, transverse momentum, rapidity, and event-shape observables affect J/ψ production.
  • Comparing the roles of multi-parton interactions, colour reconnection, and hadronisation across PYTHIA8 and PACIEA4.
  • Building Python-based frameworks for event selection, invariant-mass reconstruction, cross-section normalisation, and publication-quality visualisation.
2025

Dissertation — Nanoparticle-Enhanced 5CB Liquid Crystals

Advisor: Dr. S. P. Singh, Sharda University · with Dr. Pankaj Tripathi
  • Investigated how MgO, quantum-dot, and metallic nanoparticle doping affects dielectric anisotropy and electro-optical response in nematic 5CB.
  • Analysed changes in response time, molecular alignment, and optical behaviour under applied electric fields.
  • Characterised concentration-dependent effects of nanoparticle inclusion on electro-optical efficiency.
2024

Electro-Optical Behaviour of Liquid Crystals in Pharmaceutical Applications

Advisor: Dr. S. P. Singh
  • Examined dielectric anisotropy and optical response of liquid crystals under external electric fields.
  • Analysed nematic, smectic, and cholesteric phase behaviour in biomedical contexts.
  • Explored applications in drug delivery, biosensing, thermography, and controlled-release systems.
2023

Term Paper — Dielectric Properties & Phase Transitions in Liquid Crystals

Advisor: Dr. S. P. Singh
  • Investigated dielectric anisotropy in thermotropic and lyotropic liquid crystals.
  • Analysed phase transitions using the Onsager and Maier–Saupe models.
  • Applied theoretical frameworks to photonics, display technology, and nanotechnology.
